# Design Analysis Report: Express Water Websites

## 1. Layout Analysis

### EcoExpressWater.com
- **Hero Section**: Features a large-scale factory background image emphasizing "Professional Manufacturer in China." Strong H1/H2 text overlay for immediate value proposition.
- **Navigation**: Sticky top navigation with clear B2B categories: Home, About Us, OEM/ODM, Product, Contact Us. Includes a prominent "YouTube Video" CTA in red.
- **Product Display**: Home page utilizes a grid layout with large, high-resolution product thumbnails. Internal product pages provide detailed technical specifications and gallery views.
- **Footer**: Multi-column widget layout containing contact info (WhatsApp/Email), quick links, and WeChat QR code for direct communication.

### ExpressWater.cn
- **General Structure**: Focuses heavily on technical specifications and certifications (NSF).
- **Navigation**: Similar hierarchy to the .com site but optimized for domestic/specialized inquiry.
- **Content Focus**: Highlights OEM/ODM capabilities and the technical "why choose us" value.

## 2. Design Language

- **Colors**:
  - **Primary**: Deep Blue (#003366 approx) and White.
  - **Accent**: Vibrant Red (#E95451) used for buttons and critical CTAs.
- **Typography**:
  - Clean, modern Sans-serif fonts (likely Arial or Open Sans) for high readability in technical contexts.
- **Visual Vibe**:
  - **Industrial & Professional**: The use of factory imagery and clean lines conveys a sense of scale and reliability.
  - **Trust-Oriented**: Prominent display of NSF certifications and "25 years of experience" messaging.

## 3. Findings & Comparison
Both sites share a cohesive brand identity but serve slightly different tactical purposes. The .com site acts as a global showroom with high visual impact, while the .cn variant (based on content) emphasizes technical compliance and manufacturing excellence for the OEM market.
