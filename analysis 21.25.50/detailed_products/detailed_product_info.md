# Solar Water Heater Machinery - Product Detailed Information

## 1. Enamel Tank Production Line
### Description
The solar enamel tank semi-automatic production line is primarily composed of four parts: semi-automatic wet enamel machines, glaze slurry conveying/stirring devices, a semi-automatic drying/firing resistance furnace, and automatic inner tank drying equipment.

### Production Stages & Components
- **Semi-automatic Enameling Machine:** Equipped with two workstations, uses PLC and human-machine touch screen technology. Cycle time: 4-6 minutes. Output: 20-30 pieces per hour.
- **Drying Design:** 12-meter total length, 3 workstations. Uses hot air circulating system with 100kw electric heater.
- **Firing Line:** U-shaped firing chamber with effective conveyor length of 3.65m. Uses high-purity ceramic refractory fibers for insulation.
- **Conveyor Line:** Intermittent operation with speed 0.3-4.5m/min.
- **Firing Hanging Tool:** Custom-made with 310S heat-resistant steel.

### Technical Specifications
- **Power Supply:** 380V, 50Hz.
- **Electric Heating (Burning):** 420kW.
- **Electric Heating (Drying):** 100kW.
- **Burning Capacity:** Approx. 650kg per hour (including hangers), ~30 pieces/hour (100L tank).
- **Temperature Range:** 700°C to 880°C.
- **Workpiece Size:** Up to Φ500mm diameter and 1500mm length.

### Features & Advantages
- **Energy Saving:** High-purity ceramic refractory fibers reduce electricity consumption.
- **Precision:** High-precision microcomputer automatic temperature controller (accuracy ±5°C).
- **Hygienic:** Enamel is non-porous and resists bacteria/impurities.
- **Corrosion Resistance:** Excellent protection due to fusion of glass layer to metal.

## 4. Solar Collectors (Flat Plate / Heat Pipe)
### Flat Plate Collector
- **Working Principle:** Absorption, conduction, and radiation. Dark absorbing material converts solar radiation into heat transferred to fluid.
- **Advantages:** Efficient, low maintenance (no moving parts), affordable, easy installation, environmentally friendly.

### Heat Pipe Collector
- **Types:** Available in 12, 15, 18, 20, 24, and 30 tubes.
- **Efficiency:** High thermal efficiency and quick heat transfer due to aluminum fins and vacuum-sealed tubes.
- **Durability:** Designed for long-term use with protection from outdoor elements.
- **Applications:** Residential and commercial hot water, space heating, and industrial processes.

## 6. Inertial Sensor System (IMU)
### Description
Inertial Measurement Units (IMUs) measure acceleration, angular velocity, and orientation in three-dimensional space using accelerometers and gyroscopes.

### Applications
- Aerospace (guidance/control systems).
- Robotics (navigation for unmanned vehicles).
- Navigation (GPS-denied environments like underground or underwater).
- Virtual Reality.

### Technical Features
- **Accuracy:** High-precision measurement with compensation for temperature, vibration, and environmental noise.
- **Operation:** 5V power supply.
- **Product Range:** Currently offering 4 specific IMU products.
