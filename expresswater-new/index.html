Frequently Asked Questions

How often should filters be replaced?

Typically, PP sediment filters are replaced every 3-6 months. Carbon filters (GAC/CTO) last 6-12 months. However, this depends on local water quality and usage volume.

What is the difference between Carbon Block and GAC?

Carbon Block (CTO) is extruded carbon that also acts as a fine sediment filter. GAC (Granular Activated Carbon) allows for higher flow rates and is excellent for removing taste and odor.

Do you offer SOE and DOE end caps?

Yes, we provide both Double Open End (DOE) and Single Open End (SOE) configurations with various O-ring options to fit different housing requirements.

Can you provide OEM branding?

Absolutely. We specialize in OEM/ODM services, including private labeling, customized packaging, and unique product specifications.

What certifications do your products have?

Our key products are NSF/ANSI certified. We also hold CE and ISO certifications for our manufacturing processes.

Where is your factory located?

We are located in Yuanhua Town, Haining City, Zhejiang Province, China, which is a major hub for water treatment manufacturing.

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ varies by product line. Generally, for filter cartridges it starts at 500-1000 units, while water dispensers can be ordered in smaller container quantities.
