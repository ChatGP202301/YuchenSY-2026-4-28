Technical Support & FAQ

What makes your Precision Heating System different?

Our Precision Heating System utilizes high-grade 304 stainless steel heating elements with multi-stage thermal sensors, ensuring consistent temperature delivery without the risk of scale buildup found in traditional tanks.

Are the carbon blocks NSF certified?

Yes, our Carbon Block CTO and GAC filters are manufactured using NSF-certified raw materials, focusing on high iodine values and specific surface areas for maximum adsorption capacity.

How often should I replace the KAMAMUTA RO membranes?

Under normal municipal water conditions, we recommend replacement every 12-24 months. The 6-Stage Electric RO System includes a smart indicator to notify you based on actual flow and TDS levels.
