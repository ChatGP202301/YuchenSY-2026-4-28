# Website Analysis: Solar Water Heater Machinery (Metatecno)

## 1. Layout and Design Summary
*   **Color Scheme**: Predominantly **Industrial Grey/Dark Slate** header with **White** text. Accents of **Vibrant Red** are used for call-to-action buttons (e.g., "LEARN MORE", "SEND MESSAGE"). Content areas use a clean white background with dark grey text.
*   **Typography**: Uses a modern, clean **Sans-Serif** font (likely Arial or a similar professional typeface), which enhances readability for technical specifications.
*   **Header Structure**: Sticky navigation bar containing the company logo ("Metatecno") on the left and a horizontal menu (About US, Product, FAQ, IMU, Contact US, Language Switcher) on the right.
*   **Footer Structure**: Minimalist footer providing the factory address, phone number, email address, and a copyright notice (1998-2022 Metatecno).
*   **Vibe**: **Professional, Industrial, and Reliable**. The site feels like a direct-to-manufacturer B2B portal, prioritizing technical information and machine demonstrations (via YouTube links) over flashy marketing.

## 2. Site Structure
*   **Home**: Overview of capabilities, core products (Pressurized Tanks), and key advantages.
*   **About US**: Company history (since 1998), global reach (India, Turkey, Mexico), and service offerings (installation, training).
*   **Product**: Comprehensive list of machinery including Enamel Tank lines, Solar Collectors (Flat Plate, Heat Pipe), and PV Mounting Structures.
*   **FAQ**: Common technical and business questions (not explored in depth but present in navigation).
*   **Inertial Sensor System (IMU)**: Specialized section for IMU sensors, indicating a secondary high-tech product line.
*   **Contact US**: Lead generation form and detailed contact information for China and Mexico offices.

### Product Categories
*   **Enamel Tank Production Line**: Trusted technology for inner tanks, fusion of glass layer to metal.
*   **Solar Water Heater Production Line**: Pressurized and non-pressurized tank lines.
*   **Solar Collectors**:
    *   Flat Plate Collectors (Absorption-based).
    *   Heat Pipe Solar Collectors (12 to 30 tubes, evacuated).
    *   Solar Commercial Collectors (for hotels, hospitals, etc.).
*   **Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Mounting Structure**: Aluminum/Stainless steel structures for flat or pitched roofs.
*   **CNC Punching Machines**: For solar water heater tank production.

### Service Information
